ILLUSTRATIVE PROCESS FLOW

From incoming groundnuts to prepared product.

The steps below are an indicative process outline. Actual equipment, layout, line capacity and operating procedures will depend on final engineering and procurement.

01 / INTAKE

Receiving & sampling

Receive lots, identify supplier details and plan incoming inspection.

02 / CLEANING

Pre-cleaning

Plan removal of foreign matter before further processing.

03 / PROCESSING

Shelling & separation

Separate kernels and shells using the selected machinery configuration.

04 / SORTING

Grading & sorting

Sort kernels to target grades and customer specifications where supported.

05 / QUALITY

Inspection

Establish suitable checks and release criteria before commercial dispatch.

06 / DISPATCH

Packing & storage

Plan packaging, storage and dispatch to protect product condition.

The plant is in the planning stage. A 5 TPH design target has been discussed for the project; it should be presented as a planned target, not a proven operational capacity.
